Consent Form

Please read the following information carefully before deciding whether to participate in this study.

Purpose of the study

The goal of this study is to investigate digital learning environments and user interactions. The study is conducted by Arina Samojlenko, Jonas Borgmann and Lukas Rene Schütte.

For any questions or complaints please contact: arina.samojlenko@uni-bielefeld.de, jonas.borgmann@uni-bielefeld.de, lukas.schuette@uni-bielefeld.de

What your participation involves

  • Creating an account (username only — no real name or email required)
  • Answering a short baseline questionnaire (demographics, prior experience, digital habits)
  • Learning with our tutoring system
  • Answering short questionnaires about your experience using our system

Study procedure

This study runs over 4 days in total.

  • Day 1: You will complete three short tests.
  • Day 2-3: You are free to engage with our learning system as often or as little as you like, including not at all, if you prefer. There is no minimum requirement during this period. We encourage you to take at least one session in that period of time.
  • Day 4: You will complete one final test, which concludes your participation.

Each test takes approximately 5 minutes, though there is no time limit. Please take as much time as you need.

Important:

Since we don't collect any contact information to send reminders (in order to protect your privacy), please set your own reminder so you don't forget to return on Day 4.

Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any point without giving a reason and without any penalty or disadvantage. You may withdraw and request deletion of your data at any time during the study by contacting one of the study conductors or by deleting your account and data yourself.

What data is collected?

  • Username (self-chosen, not linked to your real identity)
  • Questionnaire responses (demographics, prior experience, experience using our system)
  • System usage data

Data usage

  • Your data is pseudonymized (linked only to your username, not your real identity) for the duration of the study.
  • Your data will be anonymized. Individual responses will never be published or shared in a way that could identify you.
  • Data will be used only for the purposes of this research.

Your right to delete your data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time via the “Delete Account” button or by contacting one of the researchers. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered and will not be included in the analysis.

Questions or concerns

If you have any questions or concerns about this study, please do not hesitate to contact us at arina.samojlenko@uni-bielefeld.de, jonas.borgmann@uni-bielefeld.de, lukas.schuette@uni-bielefeld.de.

You will receive a debriefing at the end of the study.
Thank you for your participation!
