What this study was actually about

This study is investigating how Gamification Elements, such as streaks, scores and points, affect learning outcomes and engagement in a digital tutoring system.

You were randomly assigned to one of the two versions of the system:

  • Control Group: Without Gamification Elements
  • Intervention Group: With Gamification Elements (Daily streak, score tracking, correct answer streaks, Dashboard)

The learning content was the same for all participants.

Why we asked what we asked

  • The demographic and habit questions helped us check whether the two groups are comparable, and understand whether prior gaming experience affects how people respond to gamification. We included some unrelated questions alongside these so the purpose of the study wasn’t obvious in advance. Knowing about the gamification comparison beforehand could have changed how you engaged with the system, which would have made it harder to measure its actual effect.
  • Pre- and Post-Tests to measure whether learning outcomes differed between the two groups.
  • Questionnaires after learning sessions to measure your engagement and motivation while using our system.

Reminder about your data

  • Your responses are linked only to your username, not your identity.
  • You can still request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting the study conductors or deleting your account yourself.
  • Your data will be anonymized, meaning the results will be reported only in aggregated, non-identifiable form.
